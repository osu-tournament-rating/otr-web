# Beatmaps page multi-persona review - 2026-08-10

**Date:** 2026-08-10 · **Branch:** `feature/beatmap-redesign` · **Method:** nine independent persona reviews (five UX/audience personas, four ruleset specialists) against five live fixtures — osu!standard 46827 (flagship), taiko 2901604, catch 884085, mania 4K 2024439, mania 7K 869223 — using full-page screenshots at 1440px and 390px plus live headless interaction (hover, tap, tab, and expansion testing).

The page's architecture is fundamentally sound and every reviewer said so: the SectionCard vocabulary, the pool-row drill-down into per-match lobbies, the box-plot tooltips, and the percentile hover sentence are repeatedly cited as the best artifacts on the page, and no reviewer proposed structural redesign. The problems that matter are concentrated and heavily convergent. All nine reviewers independently flagged the mobile pools list, where fixed right-hand columns crush tournament names to ~7 characters and truncate the rank-range metadata — the single worst defect on the page. A second near-unanimous cluster: the page's depth layer (box-plot and tier tooltips) is hover-only and verifiably dead on touch, so mobile silently loses most of what makes the page deep. On the hypotheses, the reviewers converged clearly: H1 is wrong about the donut but right about its hover layer (8 partially-agree, 1 disagree); H2 is confirmed with amendments (8 agree, 1 partial — catch warns green-A-plus-red-S inverts meaning there); H3 is confirmed essentially unanimously — the freemod bar is a pixel-identical twin of the mod bar and needs a different geometry, not a different palette. The catch fixture "critical bug" reported by four reviewers was root-caused by the catch specialist: beatmap 884085 is a warmup-only map whose 23 games are all game-level Rejected (`BeatmapNotPooled`), so the empty page is correct behavior — but it exposed two real issues: an unqualified "Games" stat that counts unverified games next to verified-only neighbors, and a zero-data page that renders as seven stacked hollow cards.

## Hypothesis verdicts

### H1 — "The rank-range pie presents too much information and needs simplification": **Rejected as stated; the instinct is right about the hover layer, wrong about the donut.**

Eight reviewers voted partially-agree and one (pool-selector) outright disagreed; **none** endorsed simplifying the pie itself. The static donut — at most five ordinal slices, counts in the legend, total in the center — was repeatedly called one of the most approachable marks on the page, and the per-slice mod breakdown is "the single best pool-selector affordance on the page" (pool-selector answered a real HR-for-10k+ scenario with it in under a minute). The genuine overload is the interaction layer, verified live by four reviewers: the slice-repaint morph and the tooltip double-encode the same data; the min-w-44 tooltip physically occludes the donut center and the very slice it explains; the counts are unit-less across a silent population switch ("Open · 20 pools" over "DT 55.6% · 140" where 140 is verified scores); the morph swaps into a mod palette that collides with the rank-range legend still visible below (blue=Open/NM, green=1k+/EZ, amber=10k+/HD); and the only affordance is a 10px caption saying "Hover" to thumb users. Consensus fix: drop the repaint, keep one labeled tooltip offset off the ring, add units, fix the caption — and collapse the degenerate single-bracket case (mania 7K renders a solid blue ring saying "Open 3") to a stat line. Taiko and catch push back on writing off non-osu! donuts entirely: their fixtures had real bracket spread (4 of 7 taiko pools floored; ~30% of local catch tournaments carry floors).

### H2 — "Highlight A ranks in green alongside S": **Confirmed, with amendments.**

Eight agree, one partially (catch). The case is overdetermined: A is the modal grade on the flagship (56.2%) and taiko (35.5%) yet renders as anonymous mid-gray; green A matches the in-game grade color every skilled player has internalized; and the page's **own Scores-tab badges already render A green**, so the bar would be catching up to the page's existing color language. Three amendments earned broad support and should ride along: (1) the sub-A grayscale is a scrambled ramp (A=0.56, B=0.44, C=0.92) — make it monotonic; (2) C's near-white token effectively vanishes on light-mode cards (verified live); (3) S rendered in red carries failure semantics — red is the in-client D color — and clashes with the teal/gold S badge two cards below. The catch reviewer's dissent matters: on catch (and mania), S/SS is the expected good outcome — 77.3% of the catch test map was alarm-red S — so green-A-plus-red-S would _invert_ meaning there. The strongest resolution, endorsed in substance by catch, beatmap-creator, and pool-selector: recolor the whole bar to the game-canonical palette the Scores-tab badges already use, which delivers H2's intent and fixes the red-S problem in one pass.

### H3 — "Freemod picks looks nearly identical to mod distribution and needs a distinct treatment": **Confirmed near-unanimously.**

Eight agree, one partially (ruleset-osu). The two bars are literally the same component (`toModSegments → SegmentBar → SegmentLegend`), same height, same mod palette, same legend format, ~100–250px apart in the same card, displaying conflicting numbers for the same mods (flagship: HR 27.6% vs 47.5%; catch: HR 5.8% vs 75.0%; mania: NM 97.1% vs 63.6%). Multiple reviewers — including the tournament player reviewing as a power user — genuinely re-read it as a duplicated or self-contradicting chart. The irony, noted by taiko and mania: freemod is the _more_ important chart on those rulesets (44/62 taiko scores are freemod; freemod is the only place mania's HD/FL/FI variety appears), so the confusing bar is the one most worth reading. Consensus treatment: **change the geometry, never the palette** — ranked per-mod rows with count labels (echoing the pool rows' bar-in-row pattern the page already owns), plus a plain-language lead-in; the shared mod colors are the page's cross-referencing glue and recoloring would be worse. Noted dissent: ruleset-osu argues the shared mark is what makes the 27.6→47.5 comparison legible and prefers differentiation by framing (caption, indentation, per-mod delta like "HR +19.9pp when free") over a new form; the ia-density alternative — folding freemod into the main bar as an "All scores / Freemod only" Tabs toggle — satisfies both camps and reuses existing vocabulary.

## Per-reviewer appendix

### UI/UX Engineer — Information Architecture and Density (`ia-density`)

**Summary.** The page's architecture is fundamentally sound: a consistent SectionCard vocabulary, one shared segment-bar mark for every composition dataset, strong provenance captions, and a genuinely excellent expandable pool-row → games panel that nails progressive disclosure for pool selectors. The at-a-glance layer (header, banner pills, mod bar) is approachable, and depth arrives in the right order as you scroll. The main structural weaknesses are (1) a top card titled "Mod distribution" that silently contains grades, freemod picks, and rank ranges — three datasets a scanner can never find from headers; (2) a detail layer (box-plot and tier tooltips, donut slice repaint) that is hover-only and therefore invisible or inaccessible to touch users on a page whose audience is heavily mobile; and (3) a mobile pool list whose fixed right-hand columns crush tournament names to ~7 characters and clip the "Games" caption. Sparse and zero-data fixtures degrade politely per-card but stack up to seven hollow cards on a truly empty map. Verdicts: H1 partially-agree (the donut is fine, its hover layer is the overload), H2 agree, H3 agree.

**Findings.**

- **Mobile pool list crushes tournament names and clips header captions** _(mobile · major · Pools tab)_ — At 390px, the fixed right-hand cluster (mod icon w-14 + games bar/count w-22 + toggle size-7, ~172px) leaves the name column ~7 characters: live capture shows 'The M1...', 'Stage's...', 'Swede...', 'Chevy'...'. The metadata line truncates too ('2023-01-28 · 2v…'), cutting off the rank range — the single most pool-selector-relevant attribute in the row. The sort-bar eyebrow captions also overflow: 'GAMES' renders as a clipped 'G' at the right edge. Fix within the current system: below sm, drop the w-12 spark bar (keep the count), let the mod icon ride on the metadata line, and hide the Mod/Games eyebrow captions — name and rank range deserve the reclaimed width.
- **The detail layer is hover-only; touch users cannot reach it** _(mobile · major · Score distribution, Tier breakdown, donut)_ — BoxPlotRow and TierBoxPlotRow wrap rows in Radix SimpleTooltip, which does not respond to tap — verified live. All medians/quartiles/ranges/per-mod counts and tier accuracy detail are desktop-only; sr-only text serves screen readers but not sighted touch users. The donut's tooltip does open on tap, but its hint says 'Hover a slice'. Back these rows with a Popover or touch-friendly trigger on coarse pointers; swap the hint verb via a pointer media query.
- **The top card is titled 'Mod distribution' but contains four datasets** _(both · major · BeatmapDistributionsCard)_ — Grades, freemod picks, and the rank-range donut all live under the 'Mod distribution' SectionHeader; a visitor scanning section titles can never find grades or rank ranges. The header meta ('822 scores') also collides with the rank-range sub-panel's '50 pools'. The component is literally named BeatmapDistributionsCard; the title should match, and each count should sit only beside the dataset it describes.
- **Rank-range hover tooltip shows unit-less numbers across mixed populations** _(both · moderate · RankRangePie tooltip)_ — Hover on Open shows 'Open · 20 pools' then 'DT 55.6% · 140 / HR 32.5% · 82 / …'. The 140/82/24/6 are verified scores, but no unit appears — after just reading 'pools', users will read them as pools. Label-level fix: add a 'verified scores' sub-caption or unit suffix. Also the ~8-row tooltip occludes the donut center and most of the repainted slice; nudge the offset outside the ring.
- **Grade bar grayscale is a scrambled ramp; the majority grade is invisible** _(both · moderate · Grades)_ — SS=chart-1 blue, S=chart-2 red, A=oklch(0.56) gray, B=oklch(0.44) darker, C=oklch(0.92) near-white, D=muted. Lightness does not track grade order; C reads as an unfilled gap in light mode. On the flagship, A is 56.2% yet renders as undifferentiated gray. S=red carries failure semantics while the site's own S badge is teal/gold.
- **Freemod picks bar is a visual twin of the mod distribution bar** _(both · moderate · Freemod picks)_ — Identical toModSegments → SegmentBar → SegmentLegend pipeline, same palette, ~150px apart; on the flagship both lead HR-red then HD-yellow. Either fold freemod into the main bar as a segmented 'All scores / Freemod games only' toggle using the existing Tabs vocabulary, or re-form as ranked per-mod rows. Do not recolor.
- **Box-plot panels have no score axis** _(both · moderate · By mod / Score by tier)_ — Both position marks on a shared 0..maxScore scale with no axis; a pool selector cannot read 'where is 600k' without hovering row by row (impossible on touch). The 'Accuracy by tier' panel does get a min/max axis footer — reuse that exact pattern under both box-plot stacks.
- **Zero-data maps render seven stacked hollow cards with contradictory stats** _(both · moderate · whole page / activity card)_ — Catch fixture rendered live with 0 pools and 0 verified scores: seven cards each print a variant of 'No verified scores yet'. Meanwhile the activity card shows 'Games 23' under 'Never played in a verified match' with 'Pooled in 0' — three adjacent rows that contradict each other at face value. Collapse the analytics cards into one compact band; reconciling what 'Games' counts is a product decision, flagged separately.
- **Single-slice donut carries no information for non-osu! rulesets** _(both · moderate)_ — mania 7K shows a solid one-color 'Open' ring around '3 pools'. When rankSlices.length === 1, collapse to the legend line plus a sentence, or render the segment-bar mark, which degrades to a single solid bar far more gracefully than a ring.
- **Scatter is an overplotted blob at phone width** _(mobile · moderate)_ — 822 points at ZAxis range 30 and 0.65 opacity at ~358px merge into a solid mass; drop dot size/opacity as n grows below sm.
- **Tiny-sample freemod percentages presented with full confidence** _(both · minor)_ — mania 7K renders 'NM 50.0% / FL 25.0% / HD 25.0%' from 4 scores in the same authoritative bar as 280 scores. Extending the <5 threshold is a product-policy call — flagged, not prescribed.
- **Adjacent cards quote different score totals** _(both · minor)_ — 822 / 819 / 822: BeatmapScoreDistributionCard computes its meta from the filtered array. Compute from the true verified total and let the existing caption explain the delta.
- **Attributes card is not ruleset-aware** _(both · minor)_ — Static CS/AR/OD/HP against a 0–10 gauge for every ruleset; mania's CS is the key count ('Keys 4', no gauge, is the correct read); taiko CS meaningless, AR mostly irrelevant; catch OD mostly irrelevant. Label-level per-ruleset rows.
- **Bar charts keep mount animation; every furnished capture shows them blank** _(both · minor)_ — Misses/closeness/percentile keep Recharts default animation while scatter and donut disable it; all ten screenshots show empty axes. Disable animation for deterministic rendering.
- **Box-plot tooltip overlaps the card header and neighboring rows** _(desktop · minor)_ — Hovering the first (DT) row paints the tooltip over the header and the row itself; side placement or collisionPadding.
- **Scatter axes are untitled** _(both · nit)_ — X (pre-match rating) and Y (score) have no titles; a small axis label would spare first-time visitors the 'what are these numbers' beat.
